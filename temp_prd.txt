PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT
Research Improvement Plan
Automated Multi-Label ECG Classification using SE-ResNet



1.1  Current State
The existing SE-ResNet ensemble achieves a Macro-AUC of 0.932 and Macro-F1 of 0.642 on PTB-XL Fold 10, with external validation AUROC of 0.844 on Chapman-Shaoxing. While technically competent, the paper in its current form would not clear peer review at a Q1 venue due to missing comparisons, weak statistical rigour, and underdeveloped analysis of failure cases.
1.2  Goal
Produce a publication-ready manuscript that meets the methodological standards of journals such as NPJ Digital Medicine, IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering, or Journal of Biomedical Informatics, with a realistic submission target within 3–4 months.
1.3  Non-Goals
Deployment or clinical integration — this PRD covers research and manuscript only.
Real-time inference optimisation.
Collection of new proprietary ECG data.


The following table defines current performance, publication-ready targets, and the priority of each metric.


P1 = Must-have for submission  |  P2 = Strongly recommended  |  P3 = Nice-to-have


3.1  Priority 1 — Must-Have
REQ-01: SOTA Baseline Comparisons
Implement or reproduce results from at least three established methods on the identical PTB-XL Fold 10 test split, reported under the same evaluation protocol.
Ribeiro et al. (2020) — Nature Communications CNN (12-lead, 6-class). Reference implementation available on GitHub.
PhysioNet/CinC 2020 winner — Top-ranked multi-label model. Use the team's public repository.
Transformer baseline — At minimum one attention-based model (e.g. ST-MEM, CLOCS, or W2V-BERT-ECG).
Acceptance criteria: all three baselines evaluated on Fold 10 with Macro-AUC, Macro-F1, and per-class F1 reported in a unified comparison table.

REQ-02: Statistical Validation
All reported metrics must be accompanied by 95% confidence intervals. Model comparisons must include formal significance testing.
Bootstrap CIs (n=1000 resamples) on AUC, F1, and per-class F1 for the ensemble and all baselines.
DeLong's test for AUC comparisons between the ensemble and each baseline.
McNemar's test for pairwise F1 comparisons at optimal thresholds.
Acceptance criteria: every number in the results tables has a CI; p-values reported for all pairwise comparisons against baselines.

REQ-03: Minority Class Improvement (HYP)
F1-HYP of 0.418 is insufficient for publication. At least two additional strategies must be evaluated and reported.
Threshold optimisation: sweep decision threshold per class on validation set using F1-maximisation or Youden's J.
Class-weighted oversampling: SMOTE or random oversampling of HYP samples with appropriate stratification.
Two-stage training: fine-tune final layers on a class-balanced subset after standard training.
Acceptance criteria: at least one strategy yields F1-HYP >= 0.500. All attempted strategies reported in an ablation table regardless of outcome.

REQ-04: Expanded External Validation Analysis
The 9-point AUC drop on Chapman-Shaoxing must be investigated and reported as a dedicated subsection, not a single sentence.
Per-class AUC and F1 on Chapman-Shaoxing, not just aggregate AUROC.
Analysis of which classes degrade most and a hypothesis for why (demographic mismatch, label scheme differences, recording protocol).
Test whether simple input re-normalisation (using Chapman statistics) recovers performance.
Comparison of PTB-XL vs Chapman-Shaoxing dataset characteristics in a summary table (age, sex distribution, class prevalence).
Acceptance criteria: a standalone subsection (min 300 words) with per-class breakdown and at least one domain-adaptation experiment.

3.2  Priority 2 — Strongly Recommended
REQ-05: Full Component Ablation Table
The current augmentation ablation (Figure 4) is partial. A complete table must show the independent contribution of each architectural and training choice.
Row 1: Plain ResNet (no SE-blocks, BCE loss, no augmentation) — true baseline.
Row 2: + SE-blocks.
Row 3: + Focal Loss (no augmentation).
Row 4: + Mixup augmentation.
Row 5: + Ensemble (final model).
Acceptance criteria: table with Macro-AUC and Macro-F1 for all five configurations, with CIs.

REQ-06: Per-Class Calibration Analysis
ECE is currently reported as a single aggregate number. Reviewers in medical AI expect per-class reliability.
Reliability diagram for each of the five superclasses separately.
ECE reported per class, not just globally.
Discussion of which classes are over- vs under-confident and clinical implications.

REQ-07: Subgroup Analysis
PTB-XL metadata enables analysis of model equity across patient subgroups. At minimum two variables must be reported.
Age groups: <40, 40–60, 60–80, >80 years — AUC and F1 per group.
Sex: Male vs female — AUC and F1 per group, with statistical comparison.
Acceptance criteria: subgroup results in a supplementary table with discussion of any significant disparities.

REQ-08: Uncertainty Quantification
Add a lightweight uncertainty estimate to flag low-confidence predictions, which is a key clinical requirement.
Monte Carlo Dropout: enable dropout at inference, run 50 forward passes, report mean and std of predicted probabilities.
Analyse whether high-uncertainty samples correspond to misclassified or ambiguous cases.
Report the proportion of test samples with uncertainty above a threshold and their accuracy vs the confident subset.

3.3  Priority 3 — Nice-to-Have
REQ-09: Human Cardiologist Benchmark
The gold standard for clinical AI papers is comparison to human expert performance. Even citing existing literature benchmarks is better than no comparison.
Identify published cardiologist performance on PTB-XL or similar datasets.
If resources permit: brief annotation study with 2–3 clinical collaborators on a 200-sample subset of Fold 10.

REQ-10: Transformer Comparison or Integration
Transformer-based models are the current frontier in ECG AI. Including one as either a baseline or a proposed model significantly raises the paper's appeal.
Minimum: reproduce one Transformer baseline for REQ-01 and include it in comparison tables.
Stretch goal: hybrid SE-ResNet + Transformer attention head on the final representation layer.

REQ-11: Related Work Section
The paper currently has no literature review. A proper related work section of 600–800 words covering four eras of ECG AI is required for any journal submission.
Traditional ML and handcrafted features (pre-2018).
CNN-based approaches for ECG (2018–2021).
Attention and Transformer models (2021–present).
Multi-label vs single-label formulation in ECG literature.
